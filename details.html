🩺 Heart Risk Features

🎓 Education

Level of education (1=some HS, 2=HS, 3=some college, 4=college+). Higher education often links to healthier lifestyle choices.

🚭 Current Smoker

Shows whether the person smokes. Smoking greatly increases heart disease risk. Non-smokers have lower risk.

🔥 CigsPerDay

Number of cigarettes smoked per day. Ideally, this should be 0. Even <5/day can harm heart health.

💊 BPMeds

On blood pressure medication? Taking it often means a history of high BP — controlling BP is vital.

⚠️ PrevalentStroke

Has the person ever had a stroke? Past strokes increase future cardiovascular risk.

💢 PrevalentHyp

High blood pressure? Normal: <120/80 mmHg. Hypertension increases heart strain.

🍬 Diabetes

Diabetes damages blood vessels. Normal fasting glucose <100 mg/dL reduces risk.

🧈 TotChol

Total cholesterol in mg/dL. Healthy: 125–200 mg/dL. Above 240 mg/dL = high risk.

❤️ SysBP

Systolic BP (top number). Normal <120, Elevated 120–129, High 130+ mmHg.

💤 DiaBP

Diastolic BP (bottom number). Normal <80, High 80–89+ mmHg.

⚖️ BMI

Body Mass Index. Healthy: 18.5–24.9. Over 25 = overweight; 30+ = obese.

💓 HeartRate

Resting heartbeats per minute. Normal: 60–100 bpm; athletes 50–60 bpm.

🍭 Glucose

Blood sugar mg/dL. Normal fasting: 70–99, Prediabetes: 100–125, Diabetes: 126+.
